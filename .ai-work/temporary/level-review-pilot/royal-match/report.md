**Status:** pilot candidate output only; no tags are accepted or canonical.

## Evidence and limits

The manifest's `board` paths were used verbatim (for example `levels/00002/board.jpg`); no path was generated by offsetting or slicing the archive. Candidate tags are restricted to existing target-panel analysis and the source-recorded `tutorial_overlay` flag. The evidence fields identify these as derived/source-recorded signals, not independent observations. Board geometry, blockers, colors, compartments, and patterns were not classified because the images were not individually inspected.

`verified_difficulty` and `verified_strategy` are `unknown` for all rows. No local source directly verifies them, and no speculative hypotheses were added. The source research explicitly cautions that screenshots alone cannot validate difficulty or intended strategy. No later batch was started.

## Child review reconciliation

Read-only child reviews of levels **1–500** and **501–1,000** agreed on source/feature coverage: first half has 497 records and levels 1, 299, 300 are absent; second half has 500 records with no gaps. Both found the derived difficulty to be unknown and did not assert visual tags without inspecting the images. No coverage disagreement remains.

## Capability blocker

A level-by-level visual review of 997 images was not completed. Consequently this artifact is a coverage-checked, feature-backed pilot scaffold, not a completed visual taxonomy; all available levels remain unreviewable for direct image classification. Candidate tags must not be treated as validated labels.
